Newton's First Law

Motion tries to continue

An object keeps its state of rest or motion unless a net external force changes it. When the vehicle slows down, the passenger's body tends to continue moving forward.

Real Life

Why seatbelts matter

🧠 Easy memory: If something is moving, it naturally tends to keep moving until a force changes its motion.

Real Life

Empty trolley vs loaded trolley

An empty shopping trolley accelerates easily. Fill the same trolley with heavy items and the same push produces less acceleration.

🧠 Easy memory: More Force → More Acceleration.
More Mass → Less Acceleration for the same force.

Important

Equal force does not mean equal acceleration

Newton's Third Law says the interaction forces are equal and opposite. Newton's Second Law tells us acceleration also depends on mass.

Real Life

Two people on skateboards

If two people on skateboards push each other, both move apart. The lighter person usually accelerates more, even though the interaction forces are equal.

🧠 Easy memory: You push your friend. Your friend pushes you back with an equal force in the opposite direction.
